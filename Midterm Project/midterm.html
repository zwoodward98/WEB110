Early Life:

I was born in Lawrence, Kansas on February 22, 1998. I grew up in DeSoto, Kansas and went to DeSoto schools until 7th grade.
That year, I switched to the Perry-Lecomptom school district. My dad was the middle school principal so I'd just ride to school and back with him each day.

High school and post-graduation:

I attended Perry-Lecompton High School (PLHS) for all four years of high school.
During my senior year, I enlisted into the U.S. Air Force with orders for basic training over the summer.

On July 19, 2016 I went to the airport and left for basic training (BMT).
After BMT graduation, I attended the Security Forces Academy at Lackland AFB in San Antonio, Texas.

COVID-19 Era:

I served in the Air Force for a total of four years. I originally intended on serving a full forty years, but due to significant medical issues, I had to reevaluate my life plan and decided that I needed to change the direction of things. So I separated from the Air Force in November of 2019. Luckily, I was able to immediately find a new job so my income never ceased. I got a job working at KVC Health Systems. Specifically, I worked as a Behavioral Health Technician for a children psychiactric ward. This was an incredibly difficult job, but I enjoyed it.

Insert COVID-19: The metal rod stuck in the wheel spokes of my life.
Due to COVID-19 lockdowns, my job was shut down. It ended up closing entirely. Of course, everywhere else was affected by this pandemic, so I was unable to find any work for the next year and a half or so. This eventually led to me putting deep thought into what career field I wanted to enter. Seeing as I already had experience with computers, software and coding, I decided on the IT career field. I knew this meant I'd need to go to college, something I skipped to enlist. But from that point, I knew that was the career field I really wanted to do; so I began restructuring to make college possible.
